## 🎯 Why This Assistant Is Unique

Most AI assistants lock you into a specific provider or restrict what you can ask. **Wormgpt** is built for flexibility — it acts as a frontend for local models (like Llama 3 or Mistral) and allows you to define strict system prompts, enabling advanced research and coding tasks while keeping your data entirely on your machine.
